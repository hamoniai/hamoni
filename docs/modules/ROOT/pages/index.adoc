== What Hamoni is

Hamoni is an Operations-first cloud workflow platform and AI agent harness for people-led services.
It brings AI into business processes as an active participant, so people and AI
work as one workforce. Organisations use Hamoni to design, deploy and run
processes in which people and AI agents perform work, and every action is
recorded.

== Who Hamoni is for

Hamoni is for people-led organisations that perform services or create products and want to
integrate AI into their business. It is also for organisation whose existing
processes and systems are complex, distributed or difficult to change. The
primary users are business people, process owners, operations teams and the AI
agents that work with them.
